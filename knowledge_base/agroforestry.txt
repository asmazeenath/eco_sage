AGROFORESTRY

Agroforestry integrates trees with crops or other agricultural
systems.

Trees can contribute to habitat structure, landscape diversity,
microclimate regulation and soil protection.

The suitability of agroforestry depends on local climate,
water availability, soil conditions and species selection.

Relevant metrics:
tree cover
habitat diversity
soil moisture
soil organic matter
temperature
biodiversity indicators

Source:
FAO agroforestry resources.
