SOIL HEALTH

Soil organic matter is an important indicator of soil health.
Organic matter contributes to soil structure, water retention,
nutrient cycling and biological activity.

Maintaining vegetation cover and adding organic residues can
support soil biological processes and reduce erosion.

Soil moisture interacts with organic matter and climate.
Low rainfall combined with low soil moisture can increase
drought stress.

Relevant metrics:
soil organic matter
soil moisture
soil pH
water availability
soil biological activity

Source:
FAO soil and soil health resources.
