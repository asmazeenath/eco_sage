BIODIVERSITY

Biodiversity in agricultural landscapes is influenced by habitat
diversity, vegetation structure, land-use intensity and landscape
connectivity.

Increasing habitat diversity can provide resources and shelter
for organisms including pollinators and other beneficial species.

Monoculture systems generally provide less structural diversity
than diversified agricultural landscapes.

Relevant metrics:
species richness
habitat diversity
vegetation cover
habitat loss
land-use diversity

Source:
FAO biodiversity and agriculture resources.
